Job Bots

A personal job-search assistant built and used by Amit Biswas.

Job Bots is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What it does

  • Reads job-alert emails in the owner's Gmail (read-only) and checks public company career pages for new postings.
  • Records matching jobs in a Google Sheet in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • Saves job descriptions as PDFs and drafts cover letters as Google Docs in the owner's Drive.

What it never does

  • Send, delete or change any email.
  • Submit job applications.
  • Access Drive files it did not create.

Details on data handling are in the Privacy Policy.
