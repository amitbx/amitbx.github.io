Privacy Policy

Job Bots · Last updated October 27, 2026

Job Bots is a personal tool used only by its owner, Amit Biswas. It is not available to other users. This policy explains what the app accesses through Google sign-in and what happens to that data.

Google data the app accesses

  • Gmail (read-only, gmail.readonly): used only to find and read job-alert emails (for example from LinkedIn and Indeed) so job links can be extracted. The app cannot send, delete, label or modify email.
  • Google Drive (drive.file): used only to create and update the files the app itself makes: a job-tracking Google Sheet, job-description PDFs, and cover-letter Google Docs and PDFs. The app cannot see any other files in Drive.

How the data is used

Data is used solely to find job postings, track them in the owner's spreadsheet, and draft cover letters for the owner to review. It is not sold, shared for advertising, or used for any other purpose.

Where data is stored

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Job records, PDFs and cover letters are stored in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • The app has no server and keeps no database of its own.

Third-party processing

To score how well a job fits and to draft cover letters, the text of public job postings and the owner's own resume materials are sent to the Anthropic Claude API. Email contents are not sent beyond extracting job links and posting details. Anthropic's handling of API data is described in its own privacy policy.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Job Bots' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's files from Google Drive removes the stored job data.
